What to Include in an Invoice: The Complete Checklist

Missing information on an invoice is one of the top reasons payments get delayed. A client's accounts payable department can't process what they can't understand. Here's the complete checklist — every field a professional invoice needs.

Header Information

  • Your business name — Legal name, not just your personal name
  • Your contact details — Address, phone, email
  • Business registration number — Tax ID, ABN, VAT number as applicable
  • Logo — Optional but adds professionalism

Invoice Identification

  • Invoice number — Unique, sequential
  • Invoice date — When the invoice was issued
  • Due date — When payment is expected
  • Reference / PO number — If the client provided one

Client Information

  • Client company name
  • Contact person — Name and title
  • Billing address
  • Client email — Where to send the invoice

Line Items

  • Description — What was delivered (be specific)
  • Quantity — Hours, units, or flat fee
  • Unit price — Rate per hour or unit
  • Line total — Quantity x unit price

Financial Summary

  • Subtotal — Sum of all line items
  • Tax rate and amount — If applicable
  • Discounts — If any were agreed
  • Total amount due — The final number, prominently displayed

Payment Information

  • Accepted payment methods
  • Bank account details — If accepting bank transfers
  • Payment link — If using online payments
  • Late payment policy — Fees or interest for overdue payments

Additional Notes (Optional but Recommended)

  • Thank-you message
  • Warranty or guarantee information
  • Next steps or project update
