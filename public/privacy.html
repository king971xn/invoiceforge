Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Data We Collect

InvoiceForge stores your invoices and client data locally in your browser using localStorage. We do not upload, store, or access your invoice data on our servers. Your data stays on your device.

2. Payment Information

Pro subscriptions are processed entirely by Lemon Squeezy. We never see or store your credit card details. Lemon Squeezy's privacy policy applies to all payment data.

3. Analytics

We may collect anonymous usage statistics (page views, feature usage) to improve the product. No personally identifiable information is included.

4. Cookies

InvoiceForge does not use tracking cookies. A functional cookie may be set by our hosting provider (Railway) for load balancing.

5. Third-Party Services

We use Lemon Squeezy for payment processing and Railway for hosting. These services have their own privacy policies.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on GitHub.
